## Research directions

- Physics-informed AI for wind turbine structural health monitoring under sparse sensing
- Monitoring-informed fatigue assessment and remaining useful life prediction

## Research projects

### Digital Twin Engineering for Sustainable Operation of Port Infrastructure

**National Key R&D Program of China · December 2024–November 2029**  
Principal investigator: Assoc. Prof. Jilin Hou. Role: Research team member.

My contributions include hybrid physics–data modeling for dynamic load identification and response reconstruction under unknown excitations for structural monitoring and digital-twin-based state estimation.

### Structural Health Monitoring of Wind- and Seismic-Resistant Structures

**National Key R&D Program of China · September 2020–June 2021**  
Principal investigator: Assoc. Prof. Jilin Hou. Role: Research team member.

My contributions included an SHM method combining virtual constraints with computer vision, and damage identification using the additional virtual mass method.
