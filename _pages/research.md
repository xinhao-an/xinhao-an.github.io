My research connects structural dynamics with data-driven inference to estimate loads, reconstruct responses, and assess structural condition from limited measurements.

## Current research

**Physics–data hybrid modeling for inverse dynamics.** I develop methods that incorporate physical models into learning and inference for structural dynamic load identification. Topics include FRF-guided state expansion, physics-guided neural networks, and inverse-dynamics convolution operators.

**Virtual sensing and structural health monitoring.** My work includes response reconstruction under unknown excitation, structural model updating, computer vision-based damage identification, and digital-twin-based state estimation.

**Vehicle and road identification.** I investigate vehicle parameter and road roughness identification using dynamic responses, Bayesian inference, and onboard smartphone measurements.

## Research directions

- Physics-informed AI for wind turbine structural health monitoring under sparse sensing
- Monitoring-informed fatigue assessment and remaining useful life prediction
